# ICML (main track, 2027 edition)

The card of the venue preset `paperlint:icml-2027` ([`icml-2027.jsonc`](icml-2027.jsonc)): what the
venue asks for, in prose. The numbers a machine checks live in the preset; the section at the end is
generated from it. The `submit-paper` skill reads this card for the venue's specifics.

**Re-verify the call for papers every year**
([icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)): the
limits, the dates and the style file change between editions. On 2026-11-19 the call announced
«some updates to the submission process (but not the submission format)» for late November.

## The venue (2027 edition)

- **Venue**: the main track of **ICML 2027**, the Forty-fourth International Conference on Machine
  Learning, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, 31 July – 6 August 2027, in person. Position papers have a
  separate call, not published on 2026-11-19, and are not this preset.
- **Portal**: OpenReview, `https://openreview.net/group?id=ICML.cc/2027/Conference`; the site opens
  on 8 January 2027. `paperlint submission` does not reach it (it speaks to HotCRP only), so the
  preset has no `portal`.
- **Format**: «Please review Author Instructions, an Example Paper, and use the correct Style Files.
  Submitted papers that do not conform to these policies will be rejected without review.» (the
  call); «There is no support for any typesetting software other than LaTeX. All submissions must be
  anonymized and follow the required format; otherwise, they will automatically be rejected.» (the
  author instructions).
- **Double-blind**: «All submissions must be anonymized and may not contain any information with the
  intention or consequence of violating the double-blind reviewing policy.» No acknowledgements,
  grant numbers or links to public code repositories at submission (the author instructions).
- **Impact statement**, required: «a statement of the potential broader impact of their work … in a
  separate section at the end of the paper (co-located with Acknowledgements, before References),
  and does not count toward the paper page limit.» The kit's example titles it «Impact Statement».
- **AI Disclosure Statement**, required: «All submissions must include an AI Disclosure Statement.
  The statement does not count toward the paper page limit.» The policy places it «at the end of the
  paper, before or after the references»; serious failures to disclose may lead to desk rejection.
- **Submission caps and reciprocal reviewing**: no author on more than 20 submissions; each
  submission names a reciprocal reviewer; «Submissions that do not meet these requirements will be
  desk rejected.» Each submission picks a contribution type.
- **One PDF**, references and appendices included, at most 50 MB (the author instructions; the
  kit's example still says 10 MB). Supplementary material by the same deadline.
- **Camera-ready**: one extra page; `\usepackage[accepted]{icml2027}`; US letter; a lay summary in
  OpenReview. Attendance is optional: a proceedings-only paper needs one virtual registration.

## Paper kinds and limits

| kind           | main body pages | references, appendices, statements | what the call says                                                                                       |
| -------------- | --------------: | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `main`         |               8 | not counted                        | «8 pages for the main paper, with unlimited pages for references, the impact statement, and appendices.» |
| `camera-ready` |               9 | not counted                        | «The final versions of accepted papers will be allowed one extra page for the main paper.»               |

A paper names its kind in its `paperlint.json`. After acceptance the same paper switches to
`camera-ready`, and its cycle's `accepted` turns `anonymity/identity` off.

The main body is banal's body count. Measured on the kit's example with filler text: the body to
page 8 with the Impact Statement on page 9 counts 8; the Impact Statement alone filling page 9
counts 8; a three-page two-column appendix counts 8; a body running onto page 9 counts 9, onto page
11 counts 11. banal types the statements' page and the appendix pages as references. Not
`body_ends_at`: ICML's appendix headings read «A. …», never «Appendix», and that count then could
not tell where the body ends.

## What the preset encodes

- **The ICML style**, through `extends: paperlint:icml-style` — letter page, two columns, Times at
  10 pt, references at the body's size, the TeX packages the style needs
  ([`icml-style.md`](icml-style.md)). That the style file is loaded is not checked: the family's
  card says why.
- **Double-blind review** (`blind`), so `anonymity/identity` checks the PDF against the paper's
  declared `identity`. The `accepted` and `preprint` options print the authors: both are reported.
- **The impact statement** (`required_sections`): a section titled exactly «Impact Statement», as
  the kit sets it. Where it stands is not checked: the kit lets it come before or after the
  Acknowledgements.
- **Names**: `ICML`, what `tex/venue-leftover` and `paper/folder-venue-leftover` look for in another
  venue's paper, and in the text only beside a year (`mentions: "with-year"`). 🔴 Any year: a paper
  of another venue whose text says «published at ICML 2025» gets a warning, although it cites an
  ICML paper and is no leftover. A bare «ICML» is not reported, nor is the bibliography.

## Not verified, and not checked

- **The AI Disclosure Statement.** Required, but its title is not fixed — «AI Disclosure Statement»
  in the call and the policy (whose examples are a run-in paragraph), `\section*{AI Use Statement}`
  in the kit — and it may stand before or after the references. A rule asking for one exact section
  title would report correct papers, so paperlint does not check it: add it yourself.
- **The submission-process updates** the call announced for late November 2026.
- **The camera-ready date and its form fields**: not in the 2027 pages yet.
- **The submission form's fields**: not recorded; OpenReview's form needs a logged-in read.
