# ICSE (Research Track)

The card of the venue preset `paperlint:icse-2028` ([`icse-2028.jsonc`](icse-2028.jsonc)): what the venue asks for,
in prose. The numbers a machine checks live in the preset; the section at the end is generated from
it. The `submit-paper` skill reads this card for the venue's specifics.

**Re-verify the call for papers every year**
([conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2028/icse-2028-papers](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2028/icse-2028-papers)):
the template flips between editions. ICSE 2027 asked for IEEEtran («Note that IEEE format is being
used this year, whereas last year it was ACM format.»), ICSE 2028 for the ACM template.

Sources read 2026-11-19: the ICSE 2028 call above (its raw HTML, and a summarizing fetch that
agrees); the [ICSE 2028 dates page](https://conf.researchr.org/dates/icse-2028), which lists no
date yet; `https://icse2028.hotcrp.com/deadlines`, which answered «No such conference - icse2028»;
and, for the previous edition, the
[ICSE 2027 call](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-research-track) and its
portal's [deadlines page](https://icse2027.hotcrp.com/deadlines).

## The venue (2028 edition)

- **Venue**: the Research Papers track of **ICSE 2028**, the International Conference on Software
  Engineering, «Sat 29 April - Sun 7 May 2028 Waikoloa, Hawaii, United States» (the call's header).
- **Dates** (the call, «All dates are 23:59:59 AoE (UTC-12h).»), one submission cycle: «(Mandatory)
  Abstract : June 24, 2027», «Submission : July 01, 2027», «Author response period (3 days) :
  September 24-26, 2027», «Notification : October 21, 2027», «Revision due : November 18, 2027»,
  «Camera-ready (of directly accepted papers) : November 25, 2027», «Final decision (of major
  revision papers) : December 19, 2027», «Camera-ready (of accepted major revision papers) :
  January 26, 2028». The preset carries the abstract (as `registration`), the submission and the
  notification, in UTC at the end of the AoE day, with `source: "call"`. The two camera-ready days
  stay here: a paper records its own in its cycle.
- **Portal**: none yet. «Submissions will be handled through a dedicated HotCRP platform, which will
  be available soon.» The preset has no `portal` until it exists; then its deadlines page is the one
  to plan by. The previous edition's was `https://icse2027.hotcrp.com/`.
- **Format**, verbatim: «All submissions must be in PDF format and conform, at time of submission,
  to the official "ACM Primary Article Template", which can be obtained from the ACM Proceedings
  Template page. LaTeX users should use the sigconf option, as well as the review (to produce line
  numbers for easy reference by the reviewers) and anonymous (omitting author names) options.» The
  call goes on «the following LaTeX code can be placed at the start of the LaTeX document:», and the
  page holds nothing after the colon. So a submission's class line is
  `\documentclass[sigconf,review,anonymous]{acmart}`; the preset requires only `sigconf` (the
  family's line), since a camera-ready drops the other two. «Submissions must strictly conform to
  the ACM conference proceedings formatting instructions specified above. Alterations of spacing,
  font size, and other changes that deviate from the instructions may result in desk rejection
  without further review.»
- **Double-anonymous**: «The ICSE 2028 Research Track will employ a double-anonymous review process.
  Thus, no submission may reveal its authors' identities.» Names omitted, prior work cited in the
  third person, and a preprint «must avoid specifying that the manuscript was submitted to ICSE
  2028».
- **New in 2028**, from the call: «Each submitted paper must nominate one qualified potential
  reviewer at the time of submission» (reciprocal reviewing), no bidding phase, and an early
  rejection: «Papers that receive all negative initial reviews will be rejected without proceeding
  to the rebuttal and subsequent discussion phase.»
- **References**: «Submissions will be checked for hallucinated, fabricated, or unverifiable
  references. Submissions with confirmed hallucinated or fabricated references will be
  desk-rejected without review.»
- **Generative AI**: the call quotes ACM's and IEEE's policies: disclosure of AI-generated content,
  in the acknowledgements; editing «like Grammarly» needs none. No section of its own is required,
  so the preset has no `required_sections`.
- **Open science**: artifacts are not required, but «sharing is expected to be the default, and
  non-sharing needs to be justified».
- **Attendance**: «If a submission is accepted, at least one author of the paper is required to
  register for ICSE 2028 and present the paper. We are assuming that the conference will be
  in-person, and if it is virtual or hybrid, virtual presentations may be possible.»

## Paper kinds and limits

| kind       | main-text pages | pages of only references | what the call says                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------- | --------------: | -----------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `research` |              10 |                        2 | «All submissions must not exceed 10 pages for the main text, inclusive of all figures, tables, appendices, etc. Two more pages containing only references are permitted.» |

«Accepted papers will be allowed one extra page for the main text of the camera-ready version.»,
and a paper with a Major Revision decision gets «an additional page of text in a revised paper» —
neither is the submission's limit, so neither is in the preset.

🔴 **Appendices count in the ten pages**, and a page that holds anything but references is a
main-text page: the call's words are MSR 2027's, so the preset counts the same way
(`format.appendix_in_body`, see [`msr-2027.md`](msr-2027.md) and `docs/rules/format/page-limit.md`). **Load
hyperref**: an appendix after the references is found by hyperref's anchor.

## What else the preset encodes

- **The ACM template**, through `extends: paperlint:acm-sigconf` — letter paper, two columns,
  Libertine at 9 pt, acmart's TeX packages ([`acm-sigconf.md`](acm-sigconf.md)) — with `sigconf`
  required in the class line (`tex/template`).
- **Double-blind review** (`blind`), so `anonymity/identity` checks the PDF against the paper's
  declared `identity`.
- **The venue's name** is `ICSE 2028`, what `tex/venue-leftover` looks for in another venue's paper.
  A bare «ICSE» would flag every paper that names an ICSE paper in its prose, and every folder of an
  ICSE workshop paper (`paper/folder-venue-leftover`), so the name carries the edition, as
  `AGENT 2027` does.
- **Deadlines** (`deadlines`): the call's readings; there is no portal to refresh them from yet.
- **Not encoded**: `review` and `anonymous` in the class line (above); the capitalization of the
  title and headings (`tex/heading-case` stays off: the call names no style).

## Not verified

- **The class line**: the call's LaTeX code block is empty; the options above are from its prose.
- **The portal and its deadlines**: no HotCRP site yet; the portal's `"sub"` and `"blind"` could not
  be read.
- **The page counting on an acmart build**: MSR's measurement of banal's miscount was on IEEEtran;
  no acmart build was measured for ICSE.
- **The publisher and the proceedings' producer**: the call names ACM's and IEEE's policies and
  ACM's template, but not who publishes the 2028 proceedings or who produces them, so no producer
  rule (such as `pdf/last-page-balance` or `tex/heading-case`) is turned on.
- **The reviewer nomination form**: «Additional details regarding reviewer eligibility, conflicts of
  interest, and nomination requirements will be provided in the submission instructions»; no
  `submission_fields` until the form can be read.
- **Remote presentation**: only «virtual presentations may be possible» if the conference is virtual
  or hybrid.
