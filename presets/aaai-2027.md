# AAAI (Main Technical Track, 2027 edition)

The card of the venue preset `paperlint:aaai-2027` ([`aaai-2027.jsonc`](aaai-2027.jsonc)): what
the venue asks for, in prose. The numbers a machine checks live in the preset; the section at the
end is generated from it. The `submit-paper` skill reads this card for the venue's specifics.

**Re-verify the call for papers every year**
([aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/main-technical-track-call](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/main-technical-track-call/)):
the limits, the dates and the style file change between editions (AAAI-26's kit set Times through
`\usepackage{times}`; AAAI-27's style loads newtxtext and forbids `times`).

## The venue (2027 edition)

- **Venue**: the Main Technical Track of **AAAI-27**, the Forty-First AAAI Conference on Artificial
  Intelligence, Montréal, 16–23 February 2027. The AI for Social Impact track follows the main
  track's rules; the AI Alignment track has the same page limit and its own dates (abstracts 14
  August, papers 21 August 2026) and is not this preset.
- **Portal**: OpenReview, `https://openreview.net/group?id=AAAI.org/2027/Conference` («Submissions
  will not be accepted by e-mail or any other mechanism other than OpenReview»). `paperlint
submission` speaks to HotCRP only, so the preset has no `portal`.
- **Format**: «Papers must be formatted in AAAI two-column, camera-ready style; see the AAAI-27
  author kit for details. Papers must be in trouble-free, high-resolution PDF format, formatted for
  US Letter (8.5″ x 11″) paper, using Type 1 or TrueType fonts.» The call names no rejection for a
  format violation; the kit's consequences are quoted in [`aaai-press.md`](aaai-press.md), with its
  lists of forbidden packages and commands, which paperlint does not encode.
- **Double-blind**: «AAAI submissions are anonymous and must conform to the instructions (detailed
  below) for double-blind review. The authors must remove all author and affiliation information
  from their submission for review». The kit adds that the PDF's metadata «should be cleared» and
  that references «must be anonymized whenever the reader can infer that they are to the authors'
  previous work».
- **Acknowledgements and ethics**: «Acknowledgements should be omitted from papers submitted for
  review, and any ethical statements or similar considerations, if included, must be included in
  the 7 content pages.» The kit's Ethical Statement is «optional, unnumbered».
- **Reproducibility checklist**: «All authors must complete a reproducibility checklist»; it «must
  be uploaded separately from the main paper in the designated field of the submission form». It is
  not a section of the PDF, so the preset requires none.
- **Supplementary material**: a technical supplement, a multimedia archive and a code and data
  archive, due three days after the paper; «reviewers are not required to review this material».
- **Attendance**: «All presentations must be done in-person. There will be no remote presentation
  option.»
- **Generative AI**: «Authors may judiciously use generative AI tools … However, authors remain
  fully responsible for all submitted material»; prompt injection against the review system is a
  listed ethics violation.

## Dates

The call's timetable, «all deadlines are "anywhere on earth" (UTC-12)»:

| date               | what                                          |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 21 July 2026       | abstracts, 11:59 PM UTC-12                    |
| 28 July 2026       | full papers, 11:59 PM UTC-12                  |
| 31 July 2026       | supplementary material and code               |
| 24 September 2026  | notification of Phase 1 rejections            |
| 24–31 October 2026 | author feedback window (was 19–25 October)    |
| 30 November 2026   | notification of final acceptance or rejection |
| 14 December 2026   | camera-ready files                            |

**Two readings of the abstract and the paper deadlines, both in the preset** (`deadlines`): the
call's «11:59 PM UTC-12»; OpenReview's Submission invitation `duedate` (2026-07-22 11:59 UTC) and
its venue data, «Abstract Registration: Jul 22 2026 11:59AM UTC-0, Submission Deadline: Jul 29
2026 11:59AM UTC-0». They agree to the minute. The camera-ready, a date with no hour, is the end of
14 December AoE.

## Paper kinds and limits

| kind   | body pages | pages of references | what the call says                                                                                                                                             |
| ------ | ---------: | ------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `main` |          7 |        2 (9 in all) | «Submissions are limited to 7 pages of main content, with a maximum total length of 9 pages. Any pages beyond page 7 are reserved exclusively for references.» |

The body pages are banal's count. An appendix before the references is body, as the kit asks
(«Content Appendices … count towards page limits»).

🔴 **What the page count cannot express.** An appendix placed after the references, within the nine
pages, passes: banal types those pages as bibliography (measured: 7 body pages, then the references
and an appendix on pages 8–9, reported as 7 + 2). `body_ends_at` cannot help: it finds an appendix
through hyperref, and the style refuses hyperref. Pages 8–9 are «reserved exclusively for
references»: check that by eye.

## What the preset encodes

- **The AAAI Press style**, through `extends: paperlint:aaai-press` — letter page, two columns,
  Times (newtxtext) at 10 pt, references at 9 to 10 pt, the two template lines, the TeX packages
  ([`aaai-press.md`](aaai-press.md)).
- **Double-blind review** (`blind`), so `anonymity/identity` checks the PDF against the paper's
  declared `identity`.
- **Names**: `AAAI`, what `tex/venue-leftover` and `paper/folder-venue-leftover` look for in another
  venue's paper, and in the text only beside a year (`mentions: "with-year"`): «AAAI 2027»,
  «AAAI'27», «AAAI~2027». 🔴 Not the call's own «AAAI-27»: a hyphen is not a year beside the name.
  And any year: a paper of another venue whose text says «at AAAI 2024» gets a warning.

## Not verified

- **The camera-ready's page limit and charges**: not in the call.
- **The submission form's fields**: not recorded; OpenReview's form needs a logged-in read.
- **The PDF's metadata**: the kit asks for it to be cleared; `anonymity/identity` reads the metadata
  for the declared identity only.
