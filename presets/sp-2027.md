# IEEE S&P (IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy, "Oakland")

The card of the venue preset `paperlint:sp-2027` ([`sp-2027.jsonc`](sp-2027.jsonc)): what the venue asks for, in
prose. The numbers a machine checks live in the preset; the section at the end is generated from
it. The `submit-paper` skill reads this card for the venue's specifics.

**Re-verify the call for papers every year**
([sp2027.ieee-security.org/cfpapers.html](https://sp2027.ieee-security.org/cfpapers.html)). Sources
read 2026-11-19: that page, [the home page](https://sp2027.ieee-security.org/index.html), and the
public deadlines pages of the two portals,
[cycle 1](https://cycle1.sp2027.ieee-security.org/deadlines) and
[cycle 2](https://cycle2.sp2027.ieee-security.org/deadlines). The call holds the dates; there is no
separate dates page. No S&P 2028 call was found that day (a web search found none, and
`sp2028.ieee-security.org` could not be reached), so this is the 2027 edition, **whose two paper
deadlines had both passed** when it was read.

## The venue (2027 edition, the 48th)

- **Venue**: the research papers (and SoK papers) of the **48th IEEE Symposium on Security and
  Privacy**, «May 17-19, 2027» in Montreal, the workshops on May 20 (the home page).
- **Format**, verbatim from the call: «Papers must be formatted for US letter (not A4) size paper.
  All submissions must use the IEEE "compsoc" conference proceedings template. LaTeX submissions
  using the IEEE templates must use IEEEtran.cls version 1.8b with options "conference,compsoc."
  (That is, begin your LaTeX document with the line \documentclass[conference,compsoc]{IEEEtran}.)»
  and «Papers that fail to use the "compsoc" template (including using the non-compsoc IEEE
  conference template), modify margins, font, or line spacing, or use egregious space scrunching are
  subject to rejection without review.» It adds: «some LaTeX packages (e.g., \usepackage{usenix})
  override the compsoc formatting and must be removed.»
- **Double-blind**: «Papers must be submitted in a form suitable for anonymous review: no author
  names or affiliations (whether they are real or the default fake names included in the IEEE
  template) may appear on the title page, and papers should avoid revealing authors' identity in
  the text.» Also: no identifying acknowledgments, own work cited in the third person, anonymized
  artifact repositories, and «Submitted papers should not include full CVE identifiers in order to
  preserve the anonymity of the submission.» «Papers that are not properly anonymized may be
  rejected without review.» Both portals say `"blind":true`.
- **Portals**: HotCRP, one site per cycle — «First deadline: https://cycle1.sp2027.ieee-security.org/»
  and «Second deadline: https://cycle2.sp2027.ieee-security.org». The preset names the second.
- **Submission cap**: «Any author may submit no more than 6 papers per cycle.»
- **ORCID**: «All authors are required to submit an ORCID number at abstract submission time.»
- **Server crashes**: «The authors are responsible for having a draft submitted 24 hours before the
  deadline.»

planned, we may need to adjust parts of the process in order to protect review quality. These
changes may include (but are not limited to): Adjusting dates including the early rejection
notifications, rebuttal period and final decision notifications.»

## Paper kinds and limits

| kind       | text pages | references and appendices | total | what the call says                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | ---------: | ------------------------: | ----: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `research` |         13 |                         5 |    18 | «Submitted papers may include up to 13 pages of text and up to 5 pages for references and appendices, totaling no more than 18 pages. All text and figures past page 13 must be clearly marked as part of the appendix.» |
| `sok`      |         13 |   references: not counted |     — | «For SoK papers, the references do not count towards the number of pages.» SoK papers are «distinguished by the prefix "SoK:" in the title and a checkbox on the submission form».                                       |

«Papers that are over the allowed number of pages will be rejected without review.» «Reviewers are
not required to read appendices.» «The final camera-ready paper must be no more than 18 pages,
although, at the PC chairs' discretion, additional pages may be allowed.» — the camera-ready's, not
the submission's, so not in the preset.

🔴 **The 13 pages are the text before the references and the appendix**, whichever comes first
(`format.body_ends_at: "references"`), and the page they end on counts when text stands above the
heading there. banal's count would call any page with non-bibliography text body, so an appendix
would push a correct paper over (measured for AIDC on the same template, `aidc-2026.jsonc`). **Load
hyperref**: without it an appendix placed before the references is counted as text
(`docs/rules/format/page-limit.md`). The five pages of references and appendices are judged on
banal's count of reference pages, and the 18 pages on the PDF's page count.

## What else the preset encodes

- **The IEEE template**, through `extends: paperlint:ieee-conference` — letter paper, two columns,
  Times at 10 pt, IEEEtran's TeX packages ([`ieee-conference.md`](ieee-conference.md)) — with
  `conference` and `compsoc` required in the class line (`tex/template`) and compsoc's text block,
  7.00 × 9.02 in.
- **Double-blind review** (`blind`), so `anonymity/identity` checks the PDF against the paper's
  declared `identity`.
- **Where submissions go** (`portal`): the cycle-2 HotCRP site; `paperlint submission` reads and
  updates a paper's submission through HotCRP's API there (`/api/whoami` answered 401 «Missing
  credentials» without a token, 2026-11-19).
- **Names**: `IEEE S&P`, `S&P` and `Oakland`, what `tex/venue-leftover` looks for in another
  venue's paper — in prose only beside a year no older than the paper's own edition's year minus one («S&P 2027», «Oakland'27»),
  since security papers name S&P as the venue of work they cite, «S&P» is also a stock index and
  «Oakland» a city. «Oakland» is the call's «Since 1980 in Oakland». IEEE Xplore's bare «SP» is
  left out: `paper/folder-venue-leftover` matches folder words, case ignored, and would take any
  «sp».
- **No required section**: the «Ethics Considerations» and «Generative AI usage considerations»
  are HotCRP fields at registration, not sections of the submitted PDF. The call: «authors of
  accepted papers will be asked to add the "Ethics Considerations" section itself to their
  manuscript at the camera ready stage, where it will not count toward page limits» — a
  camera-ready requirement, so not `required_sections`.

## Not verified

- **The SoK appendices' limit**: whether a SoK paper's appendices are still held to five pages (the
  call says only that its references do not count). The preset limits a SoK paper's text to 13
  pages and nothing else.
- **The submission form's fields** (`submission_fields`): the call names the SoK checkbox, the
  «Ethics Considerations» and «Generative AI usage considerations» fields, and conflict types, but
  the form was not read logged in, so none is recorded.
- **The cycle-1 camera-ready**: the call's October 16, 2026 against the portal's 2027-10-17T11:59:59Z
  (above); not resolved by the organizers.
- **Remote presentation**: the call says «One of the authors of the accepted paper is expected to
  register and present the paper at the conference» and that each paper gets «a short talk
  presentation (e.g., 5-7 minutes, length determined based on the number of accepted papers) and
  (b) a poster presentation»; it does not say whether a remote presentation is allowed. No `talk`.
- **Heading case and the register of the body**: not encoded (no accepted S&P paper is in
  `fixtures/real-papers/accepted/`).
