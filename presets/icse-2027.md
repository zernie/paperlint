# ICSE 2027

The card of the venue preset `paperlint:icse-2027` ([`icse-2027.jsonc`](icse-2027.jsonc)).

🔴 **Only the format is checked.** This preset is minimal: it exists so the ICSE 2027 workshops
paperlint ships — [NLBSE 2027](nlbse-2027.md), [AGENT 2027](agent-2027.md) and
[AGENTVERIFY 2027](agentverify-2027.md) — can name the conference they are held at as their
`parent`. It carries the edition's name and its template family, IEEE `IEEEtran` conference
([`ieee-conference.md`](ieee-conference.md)), and no page limit, no deadline and no portal: no
paper has been written against the research track's call with paperlint, so its numbers were not
read. A paper for the research track gets the IEEE format checks and nothing the call sets.

## The edition

- **The call**:
  [conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-research-track](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-research-track),
  read 2026-11-19. «ICSE 2027 Sun 25 April - Sat 1 May 2027 Dublin, Ireland» (the page header).
- **The template is IEEE**, verbatim: «All submissions must conform to the IEEE conference
  proceedings template, specified in the IEEE Conference Proceedings Formatting Guidelines (title
  in 24pt font and full text in 10pt type, LaTeX users must use
  \documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran} without including the compsoc or compsocconf options).
  Note that IEEE format is being used this year, whereas last year it was ACM format.»
- **The template flips between editions**: ICSE 2028 asks for the ACM template
  ([`icse-2028.md`](icse-2028.md)). The preset of each edition extends the family its call names.

## Its workshops follow its format

Verified on each workshop's call, read 2026-11-19:

- NLBSE 2027: «All submissions must conform to the ICSE’27 formatting and submission
  instructions.»
- AGENTVERIFY 2027: «IEEE format in line with ICSE 2027».
- AGENT 2027: «formatted according to the IEEE conference proceedings template».

Each extends `paperlint:ieee-conference` and names `paperlint:icse-2027` as its `parent`, so «ICSE
2027» in a paper of one of them is the paper's own conference, not a leftover.
