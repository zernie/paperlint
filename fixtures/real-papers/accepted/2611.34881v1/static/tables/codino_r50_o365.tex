\noindent\textbf{In-domain and cross-domain typicality.} On COCO (Tab.~\ref{tab:codino_cd}, left block), Std.\ aug yields the largest typicality gap of all rows ($\Delta^{\text{T-A}} = 9.2$), which is consistent with the $\alpha$-sweep finding in \S\ref{app:splits-impl} that conventional augmentation does not close the gap. Mosaic and YOLOX attain the smallest COCO gaps ($6.4$ and $6.7$) but lower AP on both splits relative to Std.\ (typical AP by $7.1$ and $6.2$~p.p.\@). SCP\,+\,AAS \emph{(ours)} has the highest overall AP ($\text{AP}^{\text{val}} = 52.9$) and the highest typical and atypical AP, with $\Delta^{\text{T-A}} = 8.5$; XPaste\,+\,AAS has $\text{AP}^{\text{val}} = 51.4$ and $\Delta^{\text{T-A}} = 8.8$. On cross-domain Objects365 (right blocks), the gaps of all rows lie between $2.0$ and $3.0$ with COCO NPMI and between $3.8$ and $5.1$ with O365 NPMI, so no configuration stands out; SCP\,+\,AAS again has the highest typical and atypical AP in both blocks ($34.7 / 32.4$ and $36.4 / 32.1$), and each AAS row lowers the gap of the row it starts from by $0.1$ to $0.2$.
