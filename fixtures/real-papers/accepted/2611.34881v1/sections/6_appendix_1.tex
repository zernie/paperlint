\section{Additional Quantitative Results}
\label{app:additional_results}
\suppressfloats[t]
\noindent In this section, we report the full results behind Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments} and further analyses. We first give the full results of each family in App.~\ref{app:codino_full} to~\ref{app:dinov3_lp}: the Objects365 cross-domain splits and the per-tier STRANGE-Bench breakdown for the rows of Tabs.~\ref{tab:codino_main} to~\ref{tab:dinov3_main}, and the DINOv3 linear probe. We then show the empirical selection of AAS temperature $\beta$ in App.~\ref{app:beta_sweep} and test in App.~\ref{app:circularity} whether the gaps and the AAS gains depend on COCO NPMI or on the generator of the benchmark. We finally separate pretraining data from model capacity in App.~\ref{app:scale_ladder}, test whether models also rely on the usual spatial arrangement of a pair in App.~\ref{app:spatial-arrangement}, evaluate a target-focused baseline that inpaints the partners away in App.~\ref{app:partner_mask}, and report the spread of the AAS rows over training runs in App.~\ref{app:aas_draws}. The evaluation datasets and paired splits are defined in App.~\ref{app:exp-datasets}, and the training and evaluation recipes in App.~\ref{app:exp-recipes}.
