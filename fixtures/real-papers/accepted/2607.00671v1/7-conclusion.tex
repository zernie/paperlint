In this paper, we address the challenges of the valuable but overlooked MLNC task by introducing a novel approach to understanding and leveraging label influence correlations on graphs. 
By decomposing the influences between labels in message passing mechanism, we provide insights on analyzing and quantifying label influences during the learning process. 
Our proposed method, \model, boosts the MLNC by ensuring that positive label influences are amplified while mitigating the negative ones. 
Empirical results across comprehensive MLNC datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of \model, achieving consistent improvements in various metrics.
