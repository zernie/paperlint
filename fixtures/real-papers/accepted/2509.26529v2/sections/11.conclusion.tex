\section{Conclusion}


We propose \tool, a fault-injection-based testing and detection framework for \vcs in distributed systems. \tool leverages the novel idea of causal stitching to expose the complex fault propagation chains and detects potential \vcs due to incorrect program logic. We evaluate \tool on \NumOfSysTested popular distributed systems and find \NumOfNewVC new \vcs, \NumOfConfirmedVC of which have been confirmed with \NumOfFixedVC fixed by developers. \tool is effective with little burden on targeted users.
