\subsection{Planner and Evaluator Instructions}
\label{app:agent-instructions}

We summarize the instructions governing the Planner and
Evaluator roles in the evaluated pipeline. The full prompts
are subject to institutional disclosure restrictions.
\noindent\textbf{Planner.}
Review the available evidence summaries, identify the
findings most relevant to the investigation, and assign
focused tasks to the predefined workers. Use specific static
findings or runtime observations to guide the investigation,
and identify concrete topics for contextual retrieval.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Evaluator.}
Assess the worker findings in the context of the skill's
stated purpose. Distinguish observed actions from reported
blocked actions, and consider whether the available evidence
supports a verdict or warrants further investigation.
When issuing a verdict, provide a risk score and a structured
report with citations to supporting skill artifacts or
execution records.
