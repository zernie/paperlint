\section{Serving Engine Integration}
\label{sec:serving}

We integrate \sys{} into SGLang as a backend for paged decode attention~\cite{zheng2024sglang}.
\sys{} incorporates common optimization techniques, including split-KV, which divides long contexts across CTAs to increase parallelism.
To better understand how \sys{} benefits existing serving systems with quantized KV caches, we integrate \sys{} with OSCAR~\cite{zhou2026oscar} as a drop-in replacement for its INT2 kernel and keep OSCAR's quantization policy, rotations, and BF16 windows (\Cref{sec:eval-serving-throughput}) unchanged.
