\section{Runtime-Defense Mechanisms}
\label{app:runtime}

This appendix reports how the runtime defenses act on attacked and
legitimate tool results. The stripping and response tables cover the
first single-primitive attack per case and primitive for Claude Sonnet 4.5
and GLM-5.

Table \ref{tab:content-strips} gives how often DRIFT's injection isolator
fully or partly removes the planted content, and how often it redacts a
legitimate tool result. Table \ref{tab:runtime-agent-view} gives the judge
labels (Appendix \ref{app:annotation}) under DRIFT-iso, DRIFT-full, and
Progent.
