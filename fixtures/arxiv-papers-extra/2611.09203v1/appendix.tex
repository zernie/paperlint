\section{Judge prompts}\label{sec:app-prompts}

System message (judge experiment and LLM judges in Section~\ref{sec:stream}):

\begin{verbatim}
You are a careful fact-checker. You may be shown news reports. Decide whether the claim is true. Answer with one word: True or False.
\end{verbatim}

User message, condition ``3 copies + note'' (the note line is absent in other conditions; ``none'' shows only the claim and the question):

\begin{verbatim}
News reports:
1. [Metro Tribune] Ta Tasken was born in the city of Lima.
2. [Riverside News] Ta Tasken was born in the city of Lima.
3. [Daily Courier] Ta Tasken was born in the city of Lima.
Note: all of these reports are reprints of one and the same wire story; they are one source, not 3.

Claim: Ta Tasken was born in the city of Lima.
Is the claim true?
\end{verbatim}

The note is part of the user message, directly after the reports. In ``3 different wordings'' (``indep3'' in the code) the three reports use three different wordings of the claim; in ``3 copies among 7 others'' the seven other reports state claims about other subjects, in random order.

With the Unsure option the system message ends: ``Answer with one word: True, False, or Unsure if the evidence is not enough to decide.''

\textbf{Decoding and models.} Temperature 0. The judge experiment (Section~\ref{sec:judges}) allowed 64 answer tokens; Gemini 2.5 Pro cannot switch reasoning off and used its smallest reasoning budget (128 tokens) before the answer. The LLM judges in the learning stream (Section~\ref{sec:stream}) allowed 10 answer tokens, the setting of our first judge runs. The verdict is the first standalone ``true'' or ``false'' in the answer (with the Unsure option: the first of ``true'', ``false'', ``unsure''); answers with none count as not accepted and are reported as abstentions. Models: Ollama builds phi4 (14B) and llama3.2 (Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct), both 4-bit; Gemini API model ids gemini-2.5-flash and gemini-2.5-pro, queried between 31 October and 1 November 2026.
