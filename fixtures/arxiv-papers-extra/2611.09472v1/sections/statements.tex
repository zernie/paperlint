\section*{Ethics Statement}
Consolidating agent experience into model parameters can
preserve sensitive information, erroneous behavior, or
biases present in the training traces. Our release
verification evaluates task behavior; it does not establish
privacy protection, factual reliability, or safety.
Removing records from retrieval also does not erase
information encoded in model weights, and archived records
remain stored. Applications involving sensitive data or
consequential actions therefore require additional data
governance, safety evaluation, and human oversight.
