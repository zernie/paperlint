\section{Baseline Implementations}
\label{app:published}

All baseline scores in our comparison tables are obtained
from our own runs rather than copied from published results.
Each table identifies the evaluated backbone. References
identify the methods on which our implementations are based.

\paragraph{Baseline methods.}
Our comparisons include BALROG
\citep{balrog2026leaderboard},
ReAct \citep{yao2023react},
Reflexion \citep{shinn2023reflexion},
Mem0 \citep{chhikara2025mem0},
MemRL \citep{memrl2026},
EvolveR \citep{evolver2025},
GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath},
SkillRL \citep{skillrl2026},
EDGE \citep{xie2026edge},
and MAGE \citep{yang2026mage}.
QA comparisons additionally include chain-of-thought
prompting \citep{wei2022chain},
SuRe \citep{kim2024sure},
RPG \citep{lyu2024retrieve},
EmbQA \citep{hu-etal-2025-beyond},
and RoleRAG \citep{zhu-etal-2025-single}.
For financial reasoning, we include adaptations based on
FinQANet \citep{chen2021finqa}
and APOLLO \citep{sun2024apollo}.
These references identify the source methods;
the reported scores come from our implementations.


\paragraph{Implementation and adaptation.}
We evaluate prompting, external-memory, and policy-learning
baselines under the benchmark configurations described in
Appendix~\ref{app:setup}. Method-specific components, including
reflection, memory updates, and policy optimization, are
retained in the corresponding implementations.
FinQANet-inspired and APOLLO-inspired adaptations are labeled
explicitly to distinguish them from the original systems.

\paragraph{Comparison conditions.}
We distinguish task-level baseline comparisons from
component ablations. Baseline comparisons evaluate the
implemented methods, including their respective learning
and inference procedures. Component ablations use matched
experience and task splits to examine individual design
choices in \method. Acquisition, training, and serving costs
are accounted for separately; using the same backbone does
not imply equal computational cost.
