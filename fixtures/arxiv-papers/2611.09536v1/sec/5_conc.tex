\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

In this work, we proposed a novel approach to weakly supervised video anomaly detection that incorporates external knowledge generated by a large language model and exploits cross-modal attention mechanisms. In addition, we addressed the issue of inconsistency in textual descriptions and proposed a cross-modal architecture with bidirectional attentive fusion that effectively integrates visual and textual information. Experimental results on benchmark datasets show that our approach outperforms other state-of-the-art methods. Overall, our approach provides a promising direction for advancing weakly supervised video anomaly detection using external knowledge and cross-modal modeling.
