\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{KV-cache quantization algorithms}

KIVI~\cite{liu2024kivi}, KVQuant~\cite{hooper2024kvquant}, and Kitty~\cite{xia2025kitty} establish low-bit KV formats and motivate channel-wise grouping for K and token-wise grouping for V.
Atom~\cite{zhao2024atom} and QServe~\cite{lin2025qserve} co-design 4-bit quantization formats and serving kernels, while GEAR~\cite{kang2024gear} adds low-rank and sparse error corrections.
\sys{} retains uniform affine quantization and changes how a Tensor Core kernel evaluates the quantized cache.
Nonlinear lookup formats and sparse outlier representations cannot be expressed as a single affine transform and require separate kernel support.

\subsection{Quantized attention kernels}

BitDecoding provides low-bit Tensor Core attention kernels across several GPU generations~\cite{du2026bitdecoding}.
On Hopper, it converts stored integer values and applies scales and offsets before the matrix products.
On Blackwell, it uses block-scaled FP4 instructions that restrict scales to the contraction axis, omit offsets, and quantize $Q$ and each $P$ tile to FP4.
TensorRT-LLM's XQA kernel folds a scalar INT8 or FP8 KV scale into the QK and PV accumulators~\cite{nvidia2026trtllm}.
QServe fuses low-bit KV dequantization into CUDA-core batched matrix-vector products~\cite{lin2025qserve}.
TurboAttention converts sub-8-bit values into INT8 operands for Tensor Core attention~\cite{kang2025turboattention}.
The SageAttention kernels quantize full-precision $Q$, $K$, and $V$ inputs for prefill and general attention workloads, including FP4 Tensor Core execution on Blackwell~\cite{zhang2025sageattention,zhang2025sageattention2,zhang2025sageattention3}.
SAW-INT4~\cite{jia2026saw} combines token-wise affine INT4 caches with a block-diagonal Hadamard rotation, while OSCAR~\cite{zhou2026oscar} combines a mixed INT2/BF16 cache with offline spectral rotations; both kernels reconstruct cached values before their matrix products.
\sys{} instead applies affine folding across independent token-wise or channel-wise K and V caches, folding group scales and non-zero offsets into the $Q$ or $P$ inputs or into group-local partial products while keeping $Q$ and $P$ in BF16 or FP8.
Our serving evaluation combines affine folding with OSCAR's cache policy and rotations (\Cref{sec:eval-serving-throughput}).

\subsection{Attention pipelines and kernel reparameterization}

FlashAttention-2 introduces work partitioning for tiled attention~\cite{dao2024flashattention2}, FlashAttention-3 overlaps producer and consumer warpgroups through warp specialization~\cite{shah2024flashattention3}, FlashInfer provides paged-cache attention kernels for serving engines~\cite{ye2025flashinfer}, and FlashAttention-4 studies how Blackwell's higher Tensor Core throughput shifts bottlenecks toward on-chip staging and non-matmul instructions~\cite{zadouri2026flashattention4}.
RepVGG~\cite{ding2021repvgg} folds training-time convolution branches into inference kernels, CODA~\cite{guo2026coda} rewrites Transformer operators into GEMM epilogues, and quantized GEMM kernels~\cite{zhao2024atom,lin2025qserve} rescale group partial sums or subtract zero-point activation sums in their epilogues.
\sys{} combines warp-specialized attention pipelining with scale-and-offset folding across both QK and PV under online softmax, where the eliminated per-element work grows with context length.

\subsection{Quantized inference architecture}

Oaken combines a KV quantization policy with custom hardware for online and offline compression~\cite{kim2025oaken}.
Weight-only kernels such as Marlin~\cite{frantar2025marlin,lin2025marlinw4a8}, FLUTE~\cite{guo2024flute}, and LUT-GEMM~\cite{park2024lutgemm} optimize single matrix products over quantized model weights.
\sys{} instead targets the QK and PV matrix products of decode attention, which are linked by online softmax, on unmodified Hopper and Blackwell Tensor Cores.
