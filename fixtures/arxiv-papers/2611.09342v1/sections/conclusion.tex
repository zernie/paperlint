\section{Conclusion}
In conclusion, we introduced \model, a semi-supervised framework that effectively exploits unlabelled and weakly labelled data to advance DocRE. To reduce training overhead, we employed a lightweight Self-supervised strategy that generates reliable pseudo-labels on the fly. To combat confirmation bias, we proposed a Cross-supervision mechanism that jointly trains two teacher-student networks in a collaborative manner. These components are integrated into a unified, end-to-end training procedure. Experimental results and ablation studies confirm that \model\ achieves state-of-the-art performance with improved efficiency.
