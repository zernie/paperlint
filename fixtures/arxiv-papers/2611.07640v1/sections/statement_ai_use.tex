\subsection*{AI use statement}

We used generative AI tools, principally Claude Code, for two tasks: generating and revising the synthetic benchmark dataset, and supporting qualitative analysis through trajectory annotation.

We also used these tools to find and summarize related work, create or edit
software code (including adapting existing defenses to our benchmark) and research artifacts, generate figures from author-specified
measurements, and draft, organize, and edit the paper.

We reviewed all AI-assisted work. The authors reviewed and revised every stage of case construction, and human annotators checked case validity, primitive labels, and a sample of the automatic scores (Appendix \ref{app:evaluation}). Every reported number is computed by script from the run records, and literature claims were checked against the cited papers. The authors designed the methodology, selected the analyses, and interpreted the results, and take responsibility for the final content of this work, including text, claims, code, data, and other artifacts produced with generative AI assistance.
