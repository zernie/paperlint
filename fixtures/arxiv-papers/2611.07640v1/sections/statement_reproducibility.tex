\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

Section \ref{sec:threat} defines the threat model and
Section \ref{sec:benchmark} the case design, influence primitives, attack construction, and scoring. Section \ref{sec:setup} gives the models, defenses,
and run configuration. The appendices give how cases and attacks were
constructed and filtered (Appendix \ref{app:construction}), the environments
and their tools (Appendix \ref{app:environments}), the task and attack checks
and their human validation (Appendix \ref{app:evaluation}), a complete example
case (Appendix \ref{app:examples}), the trust-hierarchy prompts
(Appendix \ref{app:trust-hierarchy}), the annotation protocol (Appendix \ref{app:annotation}), the uncertainty estimates
(Appendix \ref{app:uncertainty}), and the model versions and run settings
(Appendix \ref{app:reproducibility}). The benchmark dataset and code are available at \url{https://github.com/amazon-science/task-tampering-prompt-injection} under CC BY-NC 4.0, including the defense implementations whose
licenses permit redistribution.
